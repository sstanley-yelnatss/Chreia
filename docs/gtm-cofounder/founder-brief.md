## Core five

### 1. What it does (plain sentence)
Chreia is a local desktop app that records structured reasoning (what you believed, tried, saw, and concluded) so you can keep investigation timelines and export the *why* behind work, including for PR review. [assumption: wording; product behavior validated in repo/README]

### 2. Who it is for
Semi-technical solo founders, and people on teams who review a lot of PRs and need a faster way to understand intent without hopping on a call. Target shape: SWEs at companies, startup teammates, anyone shipping software with frequent changes who loses time reading diffs alone. [assumption: no non-friend user has confirmed this ICP yet]

**Open tension to resolve in discovery:** solo founders reviewing their own (or sparse) PRs vs team reviewers reviewing others' AI-heavy PRs are different jobs. Treat both as hypotheses until calls separate them.

### 3. What they use today instead / why you
Today, if they do anything formal, they invent and answer their own standard questions about intent in the PR. That is the only substitute pattern observed so far. Chreia's bet: capture and structure the reasoning trail so intent is already there instead of reconstructed from the diff. [assumption: substitute set and "why you" not user-validated]

### 4. Stage and traction
**0 users.** Open-source alpha, design-partner ask on GitHub, no returning-user base yet. [validated: founder report]

### 5. Strongest asset (detail)
Working demo and product (installer, MCP, capture, export, session graph / hosted viewer in recent work). Not logos, quotes, or demand metrics yet. [validated: product; assumption that demo alone converts strangers]
