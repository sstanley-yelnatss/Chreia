# Chreia GTM roadmap

**Diagnosis:** You have a real product and zero users, so the bottleneck is not messaging polish or a sales playbook. It is getting the first real conversations that prove (or kill) the PR-intent / review-time pain with people who are not your friends.

## Now

**Talk to users** (`talk-to-users`)

- Book and run **10 discovery conversations** in the next 2 weeks (syllabus target: 5 held in sprint 1 is fine; 10 is the skill bar).
- Goal of each call: confirm or reject (a) time lost reconstructing PR intent, (b) whether solo founders vs team reviewers are the real buyer, (c) what they do today instead.
- Done when: 10 non-friend conversations logged, and the brief ICP/pain lines are updated with `[validated]` or explicitly killed.

## Next

1. **Who is this for** (`who-is-this-for`) — after ~5 calls, collapse dual ICP into one primary segment and a clear "not for."
2. **Positioning and story** (`positioning-and-story`) — rewrite the wedge in *their* words; challenge the broad README line.
3. **Founder-led sales / first-50** (`founder-led-sales` + outbound syllabus) — only after pain is validated: personalized outreach + design-partner installs, not blast sequences.

## Later (parked on purpose)

- Homepage / repo blurb rewrite (`the-homepage`)
- Pricing (`pricing`)
- Launch theater (`launch-it`)
- Content engine (`founder-led-content`)
- Full DREAM dashboard (`know-if-its-working`) beyond a simple weekly scoreboard
